    Greetings! I’m currently a Master student at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), advised by Prof. Weijia Li. I received my B.E. degree from Sun Yat-sen University in 2024.

    I am currently a Tencent Qingyun Program research intern. My previous experience includes a research internship at Shanghai AI Lab mentored by Conghui He, where I contributed to synthetic data detection and optimization.

    My current research focuses on image generation and multimodal large models. My recent work involves combining AIGC detection to achieve more realistic image generation.
